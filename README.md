## Short Description

This small project had a slightly more developed background story: the tower is your body with 
your brain as a commander and granny throws candies at you to eat. However, the soldier named 
Pancreas tries to defend you from the excessive sugar. The core mechanic of the game is to 
choose the color of the soldier’s projectiles and aim for the candies that match the color to destroy them. There are 3 types of candies: red, green and blue with different behaviors (normal, 
faster and slower speed), and their HP differ as well. Each new enemy appears gradually during 
the game. By destroying an enemy, you get some XP and after you have enough XP you can use 
a special power: health regeneration.
